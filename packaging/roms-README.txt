Put your own Rad Mobile ROM zip here.

Any MAME romset of Rad Mobile works, whatever the zip is called
(radm.zip, radm_dup2.zip, a merged set with the US version inside...):
the launcher recognises the files by their contents. Older sets without
the factory EEPROM file (eeprom-radm.ic76) work too.

No ROMs are included with this package.

You can also point the launcher at another folder:
SETTINGS > ROMS > EXTRA ROM FOLDER.
